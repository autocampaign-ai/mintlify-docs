Autocampaign AI lets you text, email, WhatsApp and call your customers, reply
from one inbox, and let an AI Employee handle routine replies. You do not need
a technical background to use it. Pick what you want to do below.

## New to Autocampaign AI?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace" icon="building">
    Sign up, name your business and let the in-app checklist guide you.
    [Create your workspace](/getting-started/create-workspace) ·
    [Onboarding checklist](/getting-started/onboarding-checklist)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first message" icon="paper-plane">
    Connect a texting or email channel, import your contacts and send.
    [Quickstart](/quickstart) takes about 20 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn your way around" icon="compass">
    A tour of the sidebar, search and settings.
    [Navigate the app](/getting-started/navigate-the-app) ·
    [Glossary](/getting-started/glossary)
  </Step>
</Steps>
