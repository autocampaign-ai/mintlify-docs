## Overview

A [lead form](/getting-started/glossary#lead-form) is a form that captures leads.
We host it for you at a public link (`/f/<slug>`), and you can also add it to your
own website. It can carry your branding. Every submission is stored, matched to a
[contact](/contacts/overview), and can trigger follow-ups: tags, a segment, a
thank-you SMS or email, an opportunity, a team notification, or a workflow. Find
forms under **CRM → Lead Capture → Forms** in the sidebar (`/forms`).

<Tip>
  **In short:** 1) Build the form from a template or blank. 2) Choose what happens
  after someone submits. 3) Publish, then share the link or add it to your site.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Time to complete:** ~20 minutes &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;·&nbsp;
  Forms accept submissions only while **Published**.
</Info>

For simpler SMS sign-up pages, see [join links](/campaigns/join-links).

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- A role that lets you create and update forms. Publishing also needs a free
  published-form slot on your plan; drafts don't count.
- For a thank-you SMS, an active SMS number. For a thank-you email, a verified
  [email sender](/providers/email-senders) (an address you've proven you own).
- If you want to add submitters to a segment, create the
  [segment](/contacts/segments) first.

## Build a form

Click **Create Form** to start blank, or **Use a template** (Contact Us, Get a
Quote, Book an Appointment, Newsletter Signup, and more). Template questions stay
editable, and your work saves automatically as a draft.

questions with up to 50 choices each.

## Publish and share

The **Share** tab gives you three ways to reach visitors:

- **Hosted link** — **Copy link** or **Open form**. The slug (the last part of the
  web address) is generated from the form name.
- **QR code** — **Download QR** for print.
- **Add to your website** — choose **On the page**, **Popup**, or **Slide-in**, then
  copy the snippet (a short piece of code your web designer pastes into your site)
  for **HTML**, **React**, **WordPress**, or **Shopify**.

**Preview** (`/forms/<id>/preview`) walks through the form as a visitor would. It never
saves answers, sends follow-ups, or counts in analytics.

Forms move **Draft** → **Published** → **Archived**. **Archive** takes a form
offline and frees its plan slot; **Unarchive** republishes it. Deleting keeps
existing submissions and contacts.

## What happens on submit

Autocampaign AI saves the submission first, so a lead is never lost if a follow-up
fails. It then runs each turned-on action on its own:

- **Save as a contact** matches by phone, then email, filling only empty fields.
  Otherwise it creates a contact with source Lead Form. **Save answers to custom
  fields** stores answers that aren't mapped to a standard field on the contact.
- **Add to Audience** adds the contact to a **Segment**; **Add Tags** applies tags.
- **Send Thank You SMS** (from a number you pick) skips opted-out contacts.
  **Send Thank You Email** (from a verified sender) skips addresses on the do-not-send list.
- **Create an opportunity** in a **Pipeline** and **Stage**, optionally with an
  **Owner**. Skipped if the contact already has an open one there. See
  [Opportunities](/crm/opportunities).
- **Notify Team** sends an in-app notification.
- **Enable automations & activity log** records the submission on the contact and
  lets **Trigger Workflow** start a [workflow](/automation/workflow-builder).

A **Consent** block stores the exact consent text, whether it was checked, and the
time with the submission.

<Note>
  Consent is stored as evidence on the submission only. It doesn't change a
  contact's SMS opt-out status.
</Note>

<Note>
  There is no spam check. Each visitor is limited in how many public
  submissions they can send in a short time. Uploads are checked again for type
  and size before a submission is accepted.
</Note>

## Review submissions and analytics

The **Forms** list shows **Form views**, **Submissions**, **Conversion rate**, and
**Submissions · last 30 days**, with **Edit**, **Preview**, **Duplicate**,
**Share**, **Analytics**, **Archive**, and **Delete** per form.

A form's **Submissions** tab lists each lead with a **Status** of **New**,
**Contact created**, **Contact updated**, or **Needs attention**. Open a row for
**Responses** and **Source & device** (UTM values, referrer, page). UTM values are the
tracking tags in a link that show which ad or post the visitor came from. The row also
shows each **Follow-up action** as **Done**, **Skipped**, or **Failed**. Use **View contact**
or **Export CSV**. Submissions also appear on the contact's record.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the hosted link's slug?">
    No. It's generated from the form name when the form is created. Duplicating a
    form makes a new slug.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a submission duplicate an existing contact?">
    No. Matching is by phone, then email; only empty fields are filled and tags are
    added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a Hidden value block do?">
    It submits a fixed **Value** with every response without showing anything.
    Handy for labeling a copy of a form by where you placed it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Save & Publish blocked?">
    Fix the step named in the pop-up message, add a question a visitor can answer,
    or free a published-form slot by archiving another form or upgrading.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't the thank-you SMS or email send?">
    Check the submission's **Follow-up actions**. **Failed** usually means the sender
    number is inactive or the email sender isn't verified. **Skipped** means no
    phone or email, an opted-out contact, or an address on the do-not-send list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do visitors see 'This form is not available'?">
    The form is a draft or archived, or the link is wrong. Publish it and copy the
    link again from **Share**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
