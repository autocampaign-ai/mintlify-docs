## Overview

A lead form is a hosted, brandable form that captures leads at a public link
(`/f/<slug>`) or embedded on your website. Every submission is stored, matched to a
[contact](/contacts/overview), and can trigger follow-ups: tags, a segment, a
thank-you SMS or email, an opportunity, a team notification, or a workflow. Find
forms under **CRM → Lead Capture → Forms** in the sidebar (`/forms`).

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;·&nbsp; Forms accept submissions only while
  **Published**.
</Info>

For simpler SMS opt-in signup pages, see [join links](/campaigns/join-links).

## Before you start

- You need a role that can create and update forms. Publishing also needs a free
  published-form slot on your plan; drafts don't count.
- A thank-you SMS needs an active SMS number; a thank-you email needs a verified
  [email sender](/providers/email-senders).
- To add submitters to a segment, create the [segment](/contacts/segments) first.

## Build a form

Click **Create Form** to start blank, or **Use a template** (Contact Us, Get a
Quote, Book an Appointment, Newsletter Signup, and more). Template questions stay
editable, and your work autosaves as a draft.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basics" icon="pen">
    Set the **Lead form name**, an internal **Description**, and **Form tags** to
    organize your forms. These tags don't apply to contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Questions" icon="list">
    Add blocks from the library. **Smart Fields** (Full Name, Email Address, Phone
    Number, Company Name, Budget, and more) come pre-connected to contact fields.
    **All question blocks** adds Short answer, Long answer, Email, Phone, Company,
    Website, Address, Dropdown, Multiple choice, Checkboxes, Date, Time, Rating,
    File upload, Hidden value, Consent, and Heading, Paragraph, and Divider layout
    blocks.

    Select a question to set **Required**, **Placeholder**, **Help text**,
    **Choices**, or answer length, and use **Save answer to contact** to map it to a
    contact field or a **Custom contact detail…**. File uploads allow up to 25 MB
    and 10 files per question. **Skip ahead after this question** adds branching;
    the **Flow** view flags questions that would never be shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design" icon="palette">
    Choose a **Form style** — **Classic** (one page) or **Conversational** (one
    question at a time) — then a theme or your own **Brand color**, **Background
    color**, **Font**, **Button style**, **Logo**, and **Background image**. **Save
    current design as theme** to reuse it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After Submission" icon="bolt">
    Pick the follow-up actions (below) and write the **Success Message** or a
    redirect URL (http or https). A redirect replaces the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="rocket">
    Turn on **Publish this lead form** and click **Save & Publish**, or **Save
    Draft** to keep it private. You land on the **Share** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Publishing needs at least one answerable question. Forms hold up to 50 questions
with up to 50 choices each.

## Publish and share

The **Share** tab gives you three routes to visitors:

- **Hosted link** — **Copy link** or **Open form**. The slug is generated from the
  form name.
- **QR code** — **Download QR** for print.
- **Add to your website** — choose **On the page**, **Popup**, or **Slide-in**, then
  copy the snippet for **HTML**, **React**, **WordPress**, or **Shopify**.

**Preview** (`/forms/<id>/preview`) walks through the form as a visitor. It never
saves answers, sends follow-ups, or counts in analytics.

Forms move **Draft** → **Published** → **Archived**. **Archive** takes a form
offline and frees its plan slot; **Unarchive** republishes it. Deleting keeps
existing submissions and contacts.

## What happens on submit

Autocampaign AI saves the submission first, so a lead is never lost to a failed
follow-up, then runs each enabled action independently:

- **Save as a contact** matches by phone, then email, filling only empty fields;
  otherwise it creates a contact with source Lead Form. **Save answers to custom
  fields** stores unmapped answers on the contact.
- **Add to Audience** adds the contact to a **Segment**; **Add Tags** applies tags.
- **Send Thank You SMS** (from a number you pick) skips opted-out contacts.
  **Send Thank You Email** (from a verified sender) skips suppressed addresses.
- **Create an opportunity** in a **Pipeline** and **Stage**, optionally with an
  **Owner**; skipped if the contact already has an open one there. See
  [Opportunities](/crm/opportunities).
- **Notify Team** sends an in-app notification.
- **Enable automations & activity log** records the submission on the contact and
  lets **Trigger Workflow** start a [workflow](/automation/workflow-builder).

A **Consent** block stores the exact consent text, whether it was checked, and the
time with the submission.

<Note>
  Consent is stored as evidence on the submission only. It doesn't change a
  contact's SMS opt-out status.
</Note>

<Note>
  There is no CAPTCHA. Public submissions are rate-limited per visitor, and
  uploads are re-checked for type and size before a submission is accepted.
</Note>

## Submissions and analytics

The **Forms** list shows **Form views**, **Submissions**, **Conversion rate**, and
**Submissions · last 30 days**, with **Edit**, **Preview**, **Duplicate**,
**Share**, **Analytics**, **Archive**, and **Delete** per form.

A form's **Submissions** tab lists each lead with a **Status** of **New**,
**Contact created**, **Contact updated**, or **Needs attention**. Open a row for
**Responses**, **Source & device** (UTM values, referrer, page), and each
**Follow-up action** as **Done**, **Skipped**, or **Failed**. Use **View contact**
or **Export CSV**. Submissions also appear on the contact's record.

The **Analytics** tab covers 7, 30, or 90 days: **Views**, **Starts**,
**Submissions**, **Conversion rate**, **Abandonment**, **Views over time**, a
**Funnel**, **Devices**, **Top sources**, and **Hosted vs website**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the hosted link's slug?">
    No. It's generated from the form name at creation. Duplicating a form makes a
    new slug.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a submission duplicate an existing contact?">
    No. Matching is by phone, then email; only empty fields are filled and tags are
    appended.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a Hidden value block do?">
    It submits a fixed **Value** with every response without showing anything —
    handy for labeling a form copy by placement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Save & Publish is blocked">
    Fix the step named in the toast, add an answerable question, or free a
    published-form slot by archiving another form or upgrading.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The thank-you SMS or email didn't send">
    Check the submission's **Follow-up actions**. **Failed** usually means the sender
    number is inactive or the email sender isn't verified; **Skipped** means no
    phone or email, an opted-out contact, or a suppressed address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Visitors see 'This form is not available'">
    The form is a draft or archived, or the link is wrong. Publish it and copy the
    link again from **Share**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
