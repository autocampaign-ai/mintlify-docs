## Overview

A join keyword is a word people text to one of your SMS numbers to subscribe —
"Text `JOIN` to (your number)". When a matching text arrives, Autocampaign AI finds
or creates the contact, records the subscription, and runs the actions you chose:
add to a [segment](/contacts/segments), send a welcome message, and enroll in a
[sequence](/campaigns/sequences). Find keywords under **CRM → Engagement → Join
Keywords** in the sidebar (`/keywords`).

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;·&nbsp; Keywords match only while **Active**.
</Info>

For hosted signup pages instead, see [join links](/campaigns/join-links).

## Before you start

- Connect at least one SMS-capable number under **Settings → Channels**. Each
  keyword is bound to a number you have send access to.
- You need a role that can create keywords. Your plan may cap how many you can have.
- Segment actions need a **static** segment; sequence actions need an **Active**
  sequence.

## Create a keyword

Click **Create Join Keyword** and work through the five steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your word" icon="hashtag">
    Type the **Keyword** — letters and numbers only, up to 64 characters, stored in
    uppercase. Availability is checked as you type: **is available**, **Already in
    use on this number**, or **Reserved for compliance replies**. Suggestion chips
    (`JOIN`, `VIP`, `SALE`, …) are editable shortcuts. With more than one number,
    pick **Number customers will text**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What happens next" icon="bolt">
    Check at least one action: **Add to segment**, **Send welcome message**, or
    **Start follow-up sequence**. A sequence runs through your segment, so it locks
    **Add to segment** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose segment" icon="filter">
    Pick the **Segment** (or **Create New Segment**) and, if enabled, the
    **Follow-up sequence**. The lists filter each other to compatible pairs; saving
    adds the segment to the sequence's audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Welcome message" icon="message">
    Write the **Welcome message** (up to 1,600 characters). Keep `HELP` and `STOP`
    instructions in it; the default text already does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review" icon="check">
    Confirm the **Keyword**, **Number**, **Segment**, **Actions**, and **Welcome
    message**, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The keyword and its number can't be changed after creation. Edit the segment,
  welcome message, or sequence any time, or **Duplicate** the keyword under a new
  word.
</Note>

## How matching works

Matching is case-insensitive and considers only **Active** keywords on the number
that received the text. A message matches when the keyword is:

1. **The whole message** — `JOIN`, ` join `.
2. **The first word** — trailing punctuation is ignored, so `Join please!` matches
   `JOIN`; `VIP-1` doesn't match `VIP`.
3. **A whole word anywhere** — `Can I join?` matches `JOIN`; `rejoin` doesn't.

If several keywords match, the closer tier wins, then the longer keyword
(`VIPCLUB` beats `VIP`). A message that also contains an opt-out word (`STOP`,
`UNSUBSCRIBE`, `CANCEL`, and so on) never triggers a whole-word match.

**Reserved keywords.** Opt-out and opt-in handling always runs first, and you can't
create a keyword that collides with it: `STOP`, `STOPALL`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `CANCEL`,
`END`, `QUIT`, `HELP`, `INFO`, `START`, `YES`, `UNSTOP`, plus any custom
[opt-in](/compliance/opt-in) or [opt-out](/compliance/opt-out) keywords.

## What happens to the contact

- The contact is found by phone or created (first name "Subscriber", source Join
  Keyword). The text itself appears in the contact's [inbox](/inbox/overview) thread.
- **Opted-out contacts are declined.** A keyword never re-subscribes someone who
  texted `STOP`; they must text `START` or `YES` first.
- The subscription is recorded once per contact per keyword. A first-time join adds
  a **Keyword joined** entry to the contact's activity timeline with the keyword and
  message text as consent evidence.
- The **welcome message** is sent once per contact from the keyword's own number,
  and mirrored into the inbox thread. Repeat texts don't resend it, and it's
  skipped if the keyword is paused or deleted first.
- The contact is added to your **segment** on every matching text, so someone
  removed from the segment rejoins by texting again. Sequence enrollment follows
  from segment membership.

<Note>
  There is no double opt-in step. The keyword text is the opt-in; the welcome
  message is a confirmation, not a request to reply.
</Note>

## Manage keywords

Each keyword is **Active** or **Paused**. **Pause** stops matching (texts fall
through to your inbox), **Resume** reactivates, **Duplicate** copies the settings
under a new word, and **Delete** removes it.

The **Settings** tab holds the sharing kit: a **QR Code** (**Download PNG** /
**Download SVG**), a **Short URL**, and **Share Text** ("Text KEYWORD to NUMBER").
The QR code and short URL open the visitor's messaging app with your number and
keyword pre-filled.

## Analytics

The list opens with **New Subscribers Today**, **Active Keywords**, **Total
Subscribers**, **Opt-Out Rate**, and **Conversion Rate**, plus **Best Performing
Keywords**, **Recent Activity**, and insight cards (top converter, peak joining
window, follow-up impact) once there's enough data.

A keyword's **Analytics** tab shows **Subscribers**, **Welcome sent**,
**Replies**, **Opt-outs**, **Appointments booked**, and **Conversions**;
**Subscriber Growth** by day, week, or month; a **Conversion Funnel** (subscribed →
welcome sent → follow-up started → appointment booked → converted); and **Top
Joining Times**. **Activity** is a timeline of joins, welcome sends, sequence
starts, and bookings; **Subscribers** lists each contact with **Joined Date**,
**Status**, and **Open Contact**. "Converted" means the contact's stage is now
Customer.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the same keyword on two numbers?">
    Yes. Keywords are unique per number, so `JOIN` can run different actions on
    each number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the welcome message use merge tags?">
    No. It's sent exactly as written.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a keyword remove its subscribers?">
    No. Contacts, segment membership, and activity history are kept.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The keyword is 'Reserved for compliance replies'">
    It collides with a built-in or custom opt-in/opt-out keyword. Choose another
    word, and avoid look-alikes such as `STOPPING`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A text didn't subscribe anyone">
    Confirm the keyword is **Active**, the text went to the bound number, the
    message didn't also contain an opt-out word, and the sender isn't opted out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No welcome message arrived">
    Welcome sends need an active SMS channel and number, a non-empty message, and a
    contact who isn't opted out. They go out only on the first join.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
