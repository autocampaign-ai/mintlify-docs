## Overview

Your plan includes **credits** that are spent when you send messages and emails,
make calls, or use AI, plus **limits** on how many contacts, seats, segments, and
other items you can hold. Track both on the **Usage** tab of
**Account → Plan & Billing** (`/billing?tab=usage`), and extend them with
**add-ons** on the **Plans & subscription** tab. For choosing, changing, or
cancelling a plan, see [Plans & trials](/billing/plans-and-trials).

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Permission:** Billing read to view,
  Billing manage to purchase or change auto top-up.
</Info>

## Before you start

- You need an active subscription or trial; credits appear once one exists.
- Prices and pack sizes are shown in-app and may change.
- Purchases charge the card saved on your subscription (see
  [Payment method](/billing/plans-and-trials#payment-method-and-invoices)).

## Credits

The **Credits** card on the Usage tab shows one tile per credit pool — for
example **SMS credits**, **Email credits**, **AI credits**, and **Voice minutes**,
depending on your plan. Each tile shows the **available** balance and a breakdown:
**included · purchased · reserved**.

- **Included** credits come with your plan (subscription and promotional grants).
- **Purchased** credits come from credit packs you buy.
- **Reserved** credits are held by queued work (a campaign mid-send, a call in
  progress) and released or settled when it finishes.

SMS credits are charged per message segment and vary by destination country;
email is charged per send; AI runs and calls reserve an estimate and settle to
actual usage. Included credits can expire — expiring lots are shown in the
allocation summary on the Plans & subscription tab.

<Note>
  Pools are separate. An empty SMS pool blocks SMS sends even if you have plenty of
  AI credits. When a pool hits zero the Usage tab shows **Out of credits** with a
  **Buy credits** button, and campaigns on that channel pause until you top up.
</Note>

## Read your usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the headline">
    The Usage tab opens with your plan name, status, and **Period ends** date. A
    **Resources approaching limits** alert lists anything near its cap, with
    **Browse add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot trends">
    **30-day usage trend** charts one resource at a time (pick it from the
    dropdown) and projects usage to period end — "consider an add-on" appears if
    you're on pace to run out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare allowance to consumption">
    **Available vs consumed** lists every resource with **Plan allowance**,
    **Add-ons**, **Total**, **Consumed**, **Remaining**, **Utilization**, and a
    **Status** badge: **Healthy**, **Near limit**, **At limit**, or **Unlimited**.

## Buy an add-on or credit pack

Add-ons live on the **Plans & subscription** tab under **Add-ons**. **Your
add-ons** shows what you own as live meters; **Available to purchase** lists the
rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    Click **Purchase** on a pack. The dialog shows **You get** (units or credits)
    and **Cost** per pack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how it's charged">
    Under **How should this be charged?**, pick **One-time** (charged once, never
    re-charges) or **Recurring (auto-refill)** and set **Refill when remaining
    units reach** a threshold. Feature unlocks are one-time only.

  <Step title="Buy now">

To reload a pack you already own, click **Buy again** on its meter — one more pack
is added to your current balance.

## Auto top-up

Auto top-up re-charges your saved card for one more pack whenever the remaining
units fall to your threshold.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage">
    On an owned add-on, click **Manage** to expand its controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Switch on **Auto top-up** and set **Refill when remaining reaches**. The
    threshold is marked on the meter and shown as **Top up at …**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume if paused">
    If a charge fails, the policy shows **Paused** with the reason. Fix your card,
    then click **Resume auto top-up**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Buy more** and **Auto-recharge** buttons on the Credits card jump straight
to this section.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this a prepaid wallet I fund with cash?">
    Not exactly. Credits are included with your plan and topped up by buying
    credit packs; count limits (contacts, seats, and so on) are extended with
    capacity add-ons or a higher plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my available balance lower than the total?">
    Reserved credits are held by work in progress. They return to available (or
    settle) as soon as the send or call completes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do purchased credits expire?">
    Included grants may carry an expiry, shown in the allocation summary.
    Purchased packs show **Does not expire** or an **Expires** date on their meter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see spend by campaign?">
    Usage is tracked as consumed units per resource. **Resource events** shows the
    action and source of each consumption, not per-campaign currency spend.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Live usage temporarily unavailable">
    The usage service is briefly unreachable. Plan details still show from the
    last snapshot; retry in a moment. Nothing is charged while it's down.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A purchase asked for verification">
    Your bank requested 3-D Secure. Complete the hosted checkout to finish the
    charge; the grant arrives once payment completes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto top-up isn't refilling">
    Open **Manage** on the add-on: make sure **Auto top-up** is on and not
    **Paused**, and that your saved card is valid via **Manage plan**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A usage alert disappeared">
    Dismissed alerts stay hidden. Click **Show hidden usage alerts** at the bottom
    of the Usage tab to bring them back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
