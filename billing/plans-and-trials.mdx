## Overview

Every new workspace starts on a **card-free trial**. When you are ready, choose a
plan, and Autocampaign AI bills it monthly or yearly through a secure hosted
checkout. Manage all of it at **Account → Plan & Billing** in the sidebar
(`/billing`). The page is titled **Plan & subscription** and has three tabs:
**Plans & subscription**, **Usage**, and **Invoices**.

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Permission:** Billing read to view,
  Billing manage to change plans, buy add-ons, or cancel. Members without billing
  access don't see the sidebar entry.
</Info>

## Before you start

- Sign up from the pricing page if you can: its buttons open
  `/signup?plan=…&billing=monthly|yearly`, and the signup form shows
  **Selected plan: … · free trial — no credit card needed**. The same selection
  carries through **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Microsoft**.
- Signing up without a plan in the link starts the trial on the default plan.
  Invited teammates join an existing workspace and don't get their own trial.
- Prices, limits, and included credits are shown in-app on each plan card and may
  change. See [Credits & usage](/billing/wallet-billing) for how credits work.

## Your trial

The trial starts automatically at signup — no card, nothing to charge. Your
subscription shows the **Trialing** status, and the plan card lists **Trial ends**
with the date.

- The app header shows **Trial · N days left**, linking to Plan & Billing. It turns
  amber in the last 3 days and reads **Trial ended — choose a plan** once it's over.
- The plan card repeats the countdown with a **Choose a plan** button.
- During the trial you have the selected plan's limits and its included credits;
  track them on the **Usage** tab.

<Note>
  If the plan in your signup link is no longer offered, the trial starts on the
  default plan instead. If the trial couldn't start at all, `/billing` shows the
  plan catalog (**Choose the plan that fits your business**) so you can pick one.
</Note>

### When the trial ends

Nothing is charged. The billing page switches to **Your trial has ended** — "No
card on file, so nothing was charged — and everything you built is still here" —
above the plan catalog. Your data is preserved, but the subscription is locked:
outgoing sends are withheld and actions gated by your plan are refused until you
choose a plan.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a billing interval" icon="calendar">
    Use the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle. Plans without a yearly price show
    **Monthly billing only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose plan" icon="hand-pointer">
    Compare features, **Limits**, and **Includes … credits** on each card, then click
    **Choose plan**. The button reads **Redirecting…** while the hosted checkout
    opens. Enter your card there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the app" icon="check">
    You land back on Plan & Billing with a **Payment received** banner while your
    plan activates ("this page updates automatically in a few seconds"). If it
    takes longer, the page says so and keeps refreshing. Leaving checkout shows
    **Checkout cancelled — nothing was charged**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan picker">
    On **Plans & subscription**, click **Upgrade** on your plan card. The
    **Upgrade or change plan** dialog shows every plan; cheaper ones are labeled
    **Downgrade**, pricier ones **Upgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Upgrades apply in place and are prorated on your next invoice. You'll see
    **Plan updated — your new limits and credits apply now** or, if the change is
    still being confirmed, **Upgrade submitted**. A new checkout only opens when a
    card is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review a downgrade">
    Downgrades open **Downgrade to …?**, listing **Limits that will shrink**,
    **Features you will lose**, and reduced **Included credits**. If you're already
    over a new limit, existing items are kept but you can't add more. Click
    **Downgrade anyway** or **Keep current plan**. Downgrades take effect
    immediately; included credits are reduced pro-rata and any price difference is
    credited on your next invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment method and invoices

- Click **Manage plan** on the plan card to open the hosted billing portal, where
  you update your card. Add-on purchases charge the saved card directly.
- The **Invoices** tab lists **Invoice history** with billing period, amount, and
  status (**PAID**, **OPEN**, **VOID**, **UNCOLLECTIBLE**). Use **View invoice** or
  **Download PDF**. Invoices stay available after a subscription ends.

## Usage alerts

Alerts escalate at **70%**, **85%**, and **100%** of a limit:

- **Approaching plan limits** — "You're using most of your …" (70%) and "You're
  getting close to your … limit" (85%).
- **Resource limit reached** — "Your … are used up" (100%).

Each alert has **Buy more** and **Upgrade plan** buttons. Alerts for sending
resources (SMS, Email, Voice minutes, AI credits, Storage) appear on every page;
alerts for capacity limits (contacts, tags, segments, and so on) appear only in
the affected module and on the billing page. Only members with billing access see
them. Dismissing an alert hides it for good; restore it with **Show hidden usage
alerts** on the **Usage** tab. A **Credits running low** notification also reaches
billing members, at most once per credit pool per day.

<Note>
  A plan can set its own first warning point, in which case that replaces 70%. The
  85% and 100% rungs are fixed.
</Note>

## Out of credits

When a credit pool hits zero, the **Usage** tab shows **Out of credits** — "sends on
these channels will fail until you top up" — with a **Buy credits** button.

- **Campaigns and sequences:** the run is auto-paused rather than failed. Message
  rows show **Out of credits** (or **Payment overdue**, **Subscription inactive**,
  **Plan limit reached**, **Not included in plan**). Top up, then click
  **Resume**; held messages send from where they stopped.
- **Direct actions** (inbox replies, AI runs): the action is refused with
  **Not enough credits — buy a credit pack to continue.** Hitting a count limit
  shows **Plan limit exceeded**, and a feature outside your plan shows
  **Your plan does not include this feature — upgrade to unlock it.**
- **Overdue payment:** status becomes **Past due** or **Grace period** (with the
  end date on the plan card). Sends are withheld with
  **Your payment is overdue — update your payment method to restore service.**

## Cancel or reactivate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click **Cancel** on the plan card, then **Cancel subscription**. Your plan stays
    active until the end of the current period; the card shows **Cancels on** with
    the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your mind">
    Click **Reactivate** any time before that date and the plan renews as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the period ends, the page shows **Your subscription has ended** — "your data
is untouched" — with the plan catalog and your invoices.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a card to start?">
    No. The trial starts at signup with no card, and you're only asked for one when
    you choose a plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data deleted when the trial ends?">
    No. Everything is preserved; sending resumes once you subscribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch between monthly and yearly?">
    Yes — pick the interval in the **Upgrade or change plan** dialog. Some plans
    are monthly only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who gets usage alerts?">
    Members whose role includes billing read (Owner and Admin by default). See
    [Users & roles](/getting-started/users-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I paid but still see the plan catalog">
    Activation usually takes seconds. If the page reports a delay, wait a few
    minutes and reload; contact support if the plan never appears.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Plans or subscription details won't load">
    The page shows **Billing temporarily unavailable** or **Subscription
    unavailable** with **Retry**. Nothing was changed — try again shortly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A campaign paused on its own">
    Check the message rows' failure reason. Buy credits, update your card, or fix
    the plan limit, then **Resume** the campaign.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
