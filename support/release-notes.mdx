<Update label="September 2026" description="Help center refresh">
  ## New guides

  The help center now covers everything that shipped over the summer:

  - **WhatsApp** — connect WhatsApp Business, manage message templates, and reply
    from the inbox. See [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp).
  - **Calls** — place and receive calls, route inbound calls, and review
    recordings. See [Calls](/inbox/calls).
  - **Opportunities and pipelines** — track deals on a board or list. See
    [Opportunities](/crm/opportunities) and [Pipelines](/crm/pipelines).
  - **Lead capture** — [Lead forms](/growth/lead-forms) and
    [Join keywords](/growth/join-keywords).
  - **Sequences and experiments** — [Sequences](/campaigns/sequences) for drip
    messaging and [Experiments](/campaigns/experiments) for A/B tests.
  - **Template library** — start from ready-made templates. See
    [Template library](/campaigns/template-library).
  - **AI Employees** — replaces the earlier AI assistant guide. See
    [AI Employees](/automation/ai-employees).
  - **Analytics** — [Reports](/analytics/reports) and
    [Custom dashboards](/analytics/custom-dashboards).
  - **Agency console** — clients, setups, branding, and rebilling. See
    [Agency overview](/agency/overview).
  - **Plans and trials** — see [Plans and trials](/billing/plans-and-trials).
  - **Getting around** — the [Onboarding checklist](/getting-started/onboarding-checklist),
    [Navigate the app](/getting-started/navigate-the-app), sign-in with Google or
    Microsoft, and the [Website chat widget](/settings/chat-widget).

  ## Updated guides

  **Templates** and **Tags** moved out of Settings to their own pages, and the
  sidebar is now grouped into CRM, Communication, Marketing, Planning, and
  Insights. The guides match the new layout.
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="Documentation launch">
  ## Help center launched

  The Autocampaign AI help center is live, covering:

  - **Channels & providers** — SMS (Twilio, Telnyx) and email (verified domains and
    senders).
  - **Compliance** — A2P 10DLC, opt-in, and opt-out.
  - **Contacts** — import, custom fields, tags, and dynamic segments.
  - **Campaigns** — broadcasts, templates, polls, and links.
  - **Automation & AI** — workflow builder, AI Employees, and knowledge base.
  - **Inbox, Scheduling, Advertising, Analytics, Billing, and Developer** tools.

  Have feedback on the docs? Email [support@autocampaign.ai](mailto:support@autocampaign.ai).
</Update>
