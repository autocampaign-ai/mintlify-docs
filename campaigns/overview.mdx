## Overview

A campaign sends one message to many contacts. Autocampaign AI supports **SMS**
(including MMS with attachments) and **email** campaigns, sent immediately,
scheduled for later, or on a recurring schedule. Find them under **Marketing →
Campaigns → Text & Email Campaigns** in the sidebar (`/campaigns`). For a
timed series of messages per contact, use [Sequences](/campaigns/sequences); to
compare two versions of one campaign, add an [experiment](/campaigns/experiments).

<Note>
  Before sending SMS, connect a [provider](/channels/sms) and — for US traffic —
  complete [10DLC registration](/compliance/a2p-10dlc). Campaigns send over SMS
  and email only; [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp) messages go out through
  sequences and workflows.
</Note>

## The campaigns list

Filter by status using the tabs (each shows a live count): **All**, **Scheduled**,
**Running**, **Paused**, **Completed**, **Failed**, **Cancelled**, and by channel
with the **All channels** filter. Columns include Name, Status, Channel, Segment,
Delivery progress, Date, and Cost.

Row actions depend on status:

- **Pause** — while a campaign is Running.
- **Resume** — while a campaign is Paused.
- **Cancel** — before a campaign finishes (queued messages are cancelled; already-sent stay sent).
- **Delete** — once a campaign is Completed, Failed, or Cancelled (soft delete).

<Note>
  Campaign statuses are **Scheduled, Active (shown as "Running"), Paused,
  Completed, Failed, Cancelled**. There's no draft state — creating a campaign
  sends or schedules it.
</Note>

## Create a campaign

Click **New Campaign** (`/campaigns/new`), pick **Text message** or **Email** on
the channel screen, and click **Continue**. **Change channel** takes you back;
switching to a different channel clears channel-specific fields.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sender" icon="hashtag">
    **SMS:** select one or more phone numbers — Autocampaign AI rotates them
    round-robin across recipients. **Email:** select one or more verified
    senders. Name the campaign in the summary panel (defaults to "Untitled
    Campaign").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message" icon="pen">
    **SMS:** compose your message, insert **Variables** (merge tags), reuse a
    saved **Template**, attach media to send as MMS (the counter switches to
    "1 MMS"), and toggle **Track this link** on detected URLs. The panel shows
    the character count and SMS segments.

    **Email:** set a **Subject** (or use **Write subject with AI**), optional
    **Preview text**, and choose **Plain email** (rich text, with **AI draft**)
    or **Designed email**. For a designed email, pick **Choose a starting point**
    (**Start blank** or **My templates**) and click **Edit design** to open the
    drag-and-drop builder at `/campaigns/new/design`; your wizard progress is
    kept. Use **Send test email** to preview in a real inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Experiment" icon="flask">
    Optional. Switch on **Test two versions of this campaign** to A/B test a
    subject line or message on a slice of the audience. See
    [Campaign experiments](/campaigns/experiments) — or click **Next** to skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audience" icon="filter">
    Under **Select Segments**, pick one or more segments (up to 20). Contacts in
    several selected segments are messaged once. The panel shows **Contacts in
    segments**, who will be skipped (no phone or email, opted out, unsubscribed
    from email), and the final **Recipients** count. See
    [Segments](/contacts/segments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule" icon="clock">
    Choose **Send Now**, **Schedule for Later** (date, time, and **Timezone**),
    or **Recurring** (SMS only). Then set **Quiet hours** for this campaign —
    see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Send Campaign** in the summary panel when the status badge reads
**Ready**.

<Note>
  Segment-level excludes aren't available. Shape the audience with
  dynamic-segment rules instead.
</Note>

### Recurring campaigns

For **Recurring** (SMS only), pick a **Timezone**, then build the rule:
**Repeats** Daily, Weekly, or Monthly, **Every** N, **On days** or **Day of
month**, and **Send at**. Set a **Start date** (the first run uses the time
above) and either an **End date** or **Max runs (optional)** — one of the two
is required. The **Next occurrences** preview confirms the schedule. Every
occurrence is evaluated in the campaign's timezone, including across daylight
saving changes.

### Quiet hours

Quiet hours hold messages that come due inside a window and send them as soon
as it closes. Nothing is dropped, and the campaign still launches on time. They
apply to **Send Now**, scheduled, and recurring campaigns alike. Pick one of:

- **Use organization default** (the default) — follows the window under
  **Settings → Organization → Quiet hours**. If none is set, messages send at
  any hour and the wizard offers a **Set them up** link.
- **Set a window for this campaign** — choose **Quiet from**, **Quiet until**,
  and a **Timezone**; this overrides the organization default.
- **Ignore quiet hours** — sends at any hour even when the organization window
  is on.

<Note>
  The window is evaluated in a single timezone — the window's own, otherwise the
  campaign's schedule timezone, otherwise the organization's — not each
  contact's local time. While a campaign is inside its window, its page shows a
  **Quiet hours · resumes 9:00 AM** chip.
</Note>

### Merge tags

Personalize messages with these variables, written as `{{tag}}`:

`first_name`, `last_name`, `full_name`, `phone`, `email`, `street_address`,
`address_line_2`, `city`, `state`, `postal_code`, `country`, `full_address`,
plus `organization_name`, `organization_phone`, `organization_email`, and
`organization_website`.

You can add a fallback: `{{first_name|"there"}}` sends "there" when the field is empty.

<Warning>
  [Custom fields](/contacts/custom-fields) are **not** available as merge tags —
  only the standard fields above.
</Warning>

## Campaign analytics

Open a campaign (`/campaigns/:id`) to see its **Overview**, **Executions**, and
**Links** tabs, with **Refresh**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **End Recurrence**,
**Cancel**, and **Delete** in the header.

**Overview:** Queued, Sent (% of recipients), Delivered (% of sent), Failed
(with how many were unsubscribed), Cancelled, Total cost (SMS only — email has
no per-message cost), and click totals. Email campaigns add opens, open rate,
clicks, bounces, and unsubscribes with a delivery funnel. The **Campaign
Details** card lists the audience, schedule type, timezone, **Quiet hours**
(marked "(organization)", "(this campaign)", or "Ignored for this campaign"),
and — for recurring campaigns — last run, next run, the recurrence rule, and
upcoming occurrences shown in the campaign's timezone. Experiments show an
**Experiment results** panel here.

**Executions:** pick a run (recurring campaigns) to see its metrics — email runs
add **Opens** and **Unique opens** — and the **Messages for this run** table
with each recipient's From/To, status, engagement, and sent time. Search by
name or number, filter by status, open a recipient's **Recipient activity**
timeline, and use **Bulk actions** to **Add to segment** or **Remove from
segment**, or **Export CSV**.

**Links:** per-link clicks and click-through rate, plus top link, contact,
device, country, and browser.

Per-recipient message statuses: **Queued, Sending, Sent, Delivered,
Undelivered, Failed, Cancelled, Rejected**.

<Note>
  Reply tracking lives in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview), not on the campaign
  dashboard — the campaign view focuses on delivery, engagement, and cost.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send to more than one segment?">
    Yes — select up to 20 segments. Anyone in several of them receives the
    message once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I duplicate or re-run a campaign?">
    Duplicate isn't available as a one-click action today. Recurring campaigns
    (SMS) cover repeated sending on a schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I choose Recurring for an email campaign?">
    Recurring sending is SMS-only for now; email campaigns can be sent now or
    scheduled once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is cost estimated?">
    SMS cost is estimated per segment (a 160-character message is one segment);
    MMS is priced per message. The summary panel shows the estimate before you
    send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a reply rate?">
    Replies are handled in the unified [Inbox](/inbox/overview). The campaign
    dashboard reports delivery, cost, and (for email) engagement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Select at least one phone number' / no numbers available">
    You need an **Active**, SMS-capable number. Add or activate one in
    [Settings → Channels](/channels/sms).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'This campaign cannot be sent' on the Audience step">
    No contact in the selected segments has a phone number (SMS) or email
    address (email). Pick a different segment or add reachable contacts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages are queued but not sending">
    Check the **Quiet hours** chip on the campaign page — messages resume when
    the window closes. To bypass it next time, choose **Ignore quiet hours** in
    the wizard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SMS not delivered to US numbers">
    Confirm your [10DLC campaign](/compliance/a2p-10dlc) is approved and the sending
    number is attached to the registered messaging service.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
