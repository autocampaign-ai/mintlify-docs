## Overview

An AI Employee replies to inbound conversations in your [Inbox](/inbox/overview) on
SMS, WhatsApp, email, and the chat widget. It follows a job, works toward a goal,
answers from your [knowledge bases](/automation/knowledge-base), uses the skills you
allow, and asks a teammate for help when it should. Find employees under
**Marketing → Automation & AI → AI Employees** (`/ai-employees`).

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;·&nbsp; Only **Active** employees can reply.
</Info>

## Before you start

- Create a [knowledge base](/automation/knowledge-base) if the employee should answer
  from your documents.
- Set up [meeting types](/scheduling/meeting-types) if it should book appointments.
- Your plan limits how many employees you can have, and every reply spends
  [AI credits](/billing/wallet-billing).

## Hire an employee

Click **New Employee**. The wizard has seven steps; a draft is saved after
**Employee details**, so you can leave and **Continue hiring** later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a job" icon="briefcase">
    Start from a ready-made job — **Receptionist**, **Sales Representative**,
    **Lead Qualification Specialist**, **Customer Support**, **Appointment
    Scheduler**, **Marketing Assistant**, **Collections Officer**, **FAQ Assistant**,
    **Review Collector** — or **Custom Employee**. A job pre-fills the goal, skills,
    and human-help triggers; you can change all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Employee details" icon="id-badge">
    Set the **Name**, **Job title**, **Avatar**, **Description**, **Language**, and
    **Timezone**. Under **Voice & tone**, pick a **Communication style** (Friendly,
    Professional, Formal, Casual) and add an optional **Brand voice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goal" icon="bullseye">
    **Start from a goal** — Book Appointments, Qualify Leads, Resolve Support
    Requests, Collect Customer Information, Collect Reviews, Answer FAQs, Track
    Orders, Generate Sales Opportunities, or Custom Goal. Name it, then set **Goal is
    completed when**, **Stop trying when**, **If the goal can't be completed**, and
    **Max attempts** (1–20). Collecting goals let you list **What should this
    employee collect?** (up to 8 items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Knowledge" icon="book">
    Choose the **Attached knowledge bases**. The employee can only answer from
    knowledge bases attached here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skills" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Tick what the employee is allowed to do. **Reply to Customers** is always on.
    Available skills: **Read Customer**, **Update Customer**, **Update Custom
    Fields**, **Add Note**, **Check Availability**, **Book Appointment**, **Create
    Opportunity**, **Update Opportunity**, **Add Opportunity Note**, and **Search
    Knowledge Base**. Booking skills ask **Which meeting types can this employee
    book?** (up to 10). Skills marked **Coming soon** can't be selected yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Human help" icon="hand">
    Choose **When should this employee ask for help?** from ten triggers (for
    example *Customer asks for a human*, *Refund request*, *Negative sentiment*,
    *AI is not confident*, *Legal request*) and add **Custom phrases** (up to 25).
    Under **When help happens**, pick **Assign to** a teammate, **Leave
    conversation summary**, and **Notify team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review" icon="check">
    Confirm the summary, then click **Create employee** (saves as **Draft**) or
    **Create & activate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You don't choose an AI model. The **Overview** tab shows which one powers the
  employee under **Powered by** — it is managed by the platform.
</Note>

Activation is blocked if **Search Knowledge Base** is ticked with no knowledge base
attached, a booking skill has no meeting type, or a **Collect Reviews** goal has no
saving skill (**Add Note**, **Update Customer**, or **Update Custom Fields**).

## Put the employee on conversations

An employee only replies on conversations it's assigned to. Three ways:

- **From the inbox** — in the contact panel's **AI Employee** card, click **Assign**
  and select an employee. Use **Change** or **Unassign** later.
- **Automatically** — set **Default employee for new conversations** on the AI
  Employees page, or pick an **AI Employee** per phone number (**Channels**), per
  email sender (**Settings → Email → Senders**), or in the
  [chat widget](/settings/chat-widget). A new inbound conversation goes to the
  number's employee, then the default. A conversation a teammate already owns is
  never taken over.
- **From a workflow** — add the **Assign AI Employee** action in the
  [workflow builder](/automation/workflow-builder). The employee replies when the
  contact next messages; it doesn't send anything on its own.

## Auto-send or draft & approve

Assigning an employee puts the conversation in **AI active**: the employee sends
its own replies. To review first, open the composer's **AI Autopilot** menu and
choose **Draft & approve**. The chip reads **Autopilot: Draft & approve**, and each
reply appears as a banner — **&lt;employee&gt; needs approval** — with **Edit** and
**Approve & Send**. A newer inbound message replaces an unapproved draft.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take over">
    Click **Take over** on the AI Employee card, or just send a reply while in
    **Auto-send** mode. The conversation becomes **Human controlled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume">
    Click **Resume AI** to hand the conversation back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Draft & approve** mode, replying yourself doesn't pause the employee — it keeps
drafting the next reply for your approval.

**Conversation states:** AI active · Supervised · Handover pending · Human
controlled · AI suspended. After three consecutive failed runs a conversation is
**AI suspended** until you **Resume AI**.

### Human help in practice

When a trigger fires, the employee sends its courtesy line, the conversation goes to
**Handover pending**, the chosen teammate is assigned, the team is notified, and an
AI-written summary note is added to the thread. A **Goal** card in the contact
panel shows the checklist and lets you **Mark complete**.

## Channels and limits

- **SMS** replies are plain text, capped at 1,000 characters by default.
- **WhatsApp** replies use WhatsApp formatting. If the 24-hour customer-service
  window has closed, the reply is skipped and logged — the employee never sends
  templates.
- **Email** replies are formatted (up to 2,000 characters); an SMS opt-out doesn't
  block email replies.
- **Chat widget** replies support rich text. Opted-out contacts with no email
  address can't be assigned.
- Each reply reserves AI credits; with none left the run is skipped as **Not
  enough credits**.

## Manage employees

Each card shows today's **Conversations**, **Appointments**, **Handovers**, and
**Goal success**, with **Pause**, **Resume**, **Duplicate**, and **Delete**. The
profile has **Overview** (edit any section and **Save**), **Activity** (every
interaction — what the customer asked, the reply, actions taken, knowledge used,
and why a reply was skipped), and **Test chat**, which runs the saved configuration
without touching real conversations.

<Note>
  **Retry later** under *If the goal can't be completed* is accepted but currently
  does nothing. Employees handling active conversations can't be deleted — pause or
  reassign them first.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the employee reply to everyone automatically?">
    Only on conversations it's assigned to — manually, by a default or per-number
    assignment, or by a workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it book meetings or update my CRM?">
    Yes. Enable the booking, customer, and opportunity skills. Opportunity skills act
    only on the conversation's contact and their open deal — see
    [Opportunities](/crm/opportunities).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the composer's AI Reply the same thing?">
    No. **AI Reply** and **AI Draft** rewrite or compose text for you and need no
    employee. They also use AI credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the employee's instructions directly?">
    No. Instructions are assembled from the job, goal, skills, and voice you set.
    The **Advanced** tab is for platform staff only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The employee isn't replying">
    Check it's **Active**, the conversation shows **AI active** or **Supervised**, the
    contact isn't opted out, and you have AI credits. The **Activity** tab shows the
    skip reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Answers ignore my documents">
    Attach the knowledge base on the **Knowledge** step, tick **Search Knowledge
    Base**, and confirm ingestion finished.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create & activate fails">
    Read the validation message — usually a missing knowledge base, meeting type, or
    saving skill (see above).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
