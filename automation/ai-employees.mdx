## Overview

An [AI Employee](/getting-started/glossary#ai-employee) replies to incoming
conversations in your [Inbox](/inbox/overview) on SMS, WhatsApp, email, and the
[chat widget](/getting-started/glossary#chat-widget). It follows a job, works toward a goal, and answers from your
[knowledge bases](/automation/knowledge-base) (the documents it reads to answer
questions). It uses only the skills (actions) you allow, and asks a teammate for
help when it should. Find employees under
**Marketing → Automation & AI → AI Employees** (`/ai-employees`).

<Tip>
  **In short:** 1) Hire an employee with the seven-step wizard. 2) Assign it to
  conversations by hand, by default, or by workflow. 3) Let it send replies or
  approve its drafts first.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Time to complete:** ~20 minutes &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;·&nbsp; Only **Active** employees can reply.
</Info>

## Before you start

- Create a [knowledge base](/automation/knowledge-base) if the employee should answer
  from your documents.
- Set up [meeting types](/scheduling/meeting-types) (the kinds of appointments people
  can book) if it should book appointments.
- Your [plan](/getting-started/glossary#plan) sets how many employees you can have.
  Every reply spends [AI credits](/billing/wallet-billing) (the balance that pays
  for AI work).

## Hire an employee

Click **New Employee**. The wizard (a guided form) has seven steps. A draft is saved
after **Employee details**, so you can leave and **Continue hiring** later.

Activation is blocked when:

- **Search Knowledge Base** is ticked but no knowledge base is attached.
- A booking skill has no meeting type.
- A **Collect Reviews** goal has no saving skill (**Add Note**, **Update Customer**,
  or **Update Custom Fields**).

## Put the employee on conversations

An employee only replies on conversations it's assigned to. Three ways:

- **From the inbox** — in the contact panel's **AI Employee** card, click **Assign**
  and select an employee. Use **Change** or **Unassign** later.
- **Automatically** — set **Default employee for new conversations** on the AI
  Employees page. Or pick an **AI Employee** per phone number (**Channels**), per
  email sender (**Settings → Email → Senders**), or in the
  [chat widget](/settings/chat-widget). A new incoming conversation goes to the
  number's employee first, then to the default. A conversation a teammate already
  owns is never taken over.
- **From a workflow** (an automation that runs steps for you) — add the **Assign AI
  Employee** action in the [workflow builder](/automation/workflow-builder). The
  employee replies when the contact next messages; it doesn't send anything on its
  own.

## Choose between auto-send and draft & approve

Assigning an employee puts the conversation in **AI active**: the employee sends
its own replies. To review first, open the **AI Autopilot** menu in the composer
(the box where you type replies) and choose **Draft & approve**. The chip (small
label) reads **Autopilot: Draft & approve**, and each reply appears as a banner —
**&lt;employee&gt; needs approval** — with **Edit** and **Approve & Send**. A newer
incoming message replaces an unapproved draft.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take over">
    Click **Take over** on the AI Employee card, or just send a reply while in
    **Auto-send** mode. The conversation becomes **Human controlled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume">
    Click **Resume AI** to hand the conversation back.
  </Step>

In **Draft & approve** mode, replying yourself doesn't pause the employee — it keeps
drafting the next reply for your approval.

**Conversation states:** AI active · Supervised · Handover pending · Human
controlled · AI suspended. After three failed reply attempts in a row, a
conversation is **AI suspended** until you **Resume AI**.

### What happens when the employee asks for help

When a trigger fires, the employee sends its courtesy line and the conversation
goes to **Handover pending**. The chosen teammate is assigned, the team is
notified, and an AI-written summary note is added to the thread. A **Goal** card in
the contact panel shows the checklist and lets you **Mark complete**.

## What replies look like on each channel

- **SMS** replies are plain text, capped at 1,000 characters by default.
- **WhatsApp** replies use WhatsApp formatting. If the 24-hour customer-service
  window has closed (WhatsApp only lets you reply freely within 24 hours of the
  customer's last message), the reply is skipped and logged. The employee never
  sends templates.
- **Email** replies are formatted (up to 2,000 characters). An SMS
  [opt-out](/getting-started/glossary#opt-out) (a request to stop receiving texts)
  doesn't block email replies.
- **Chat widget** replies support rich text. Opted-out contacts with no email
  address can't be assigned.
- Each reply sets aside AI credits; with none left the reply is skipped as **Not
  enough credits**.

## Manage employees

Each card shows today's **Conversations**, **Appointments**, **Handovers**, and
**Goal success**, with **Pause**, **Resume**, **Duplicate**, and **Delete**. The
profile has three tabs. **Overview** lets you edit any section and **Save**.
**Activity** lists every interaction: what the customer asked, the reply, actions
taken, knowledge used, and why a reply was skipped. **Test chat** lets you try the
saved setup without touching real conversations.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the employee reply to everyone automatically?">
    Only on conversations it's assigned to — by hand, by a default or per-number
    assignment, or by a workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it book meetings or update my contacts and deals?">
    Yes. Turn on the booking, customer, and opportunity skills. Opportunity skills
    act only on the conversation's contact and their open deal — see
    [Opportunities](/crm/opportunities).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the AI Reply button in the composer the same thing?">
    No. **AI Reply** and **AI Draft** rewrite or compose text for you and need no
    employee. They also use AI credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the employee's instructions directly?">
    No. Instructions are assembled from the job, goal, skills, and voice you set.
    The **Advanced** tab is for platform staff only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The employee isn't replying">
    Check it's **Active**, the conversation shows **AI active** or **Supervised**, the
    contact isn't opted out, and you have AI credits. The **Activity** tab shows the
    skip reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The answers ignore my documents">
    Attach the knowledge base on the **Knowledge** step, tick **Search Knowledge
    Base**, and confirm the sources finished processing (status **Completed**).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create & activate fails with an error">
    Read the message shown — usually a missing knowledge base, meeting type, or
    saving skill (see above).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
