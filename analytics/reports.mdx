## Overview

The **Analytics** group under **Insights** in the sidebar holds six reports, each
on its own route: **Overview** (`/analytics/overview`), **Marketing**
(`/analytics/marketing`), **Conversations** (`/analytics/conversations`),
**Revenue** (`/analytics/revenue`), **Appointments** (`/analytics/appointments`),
and **Deliverability** (`/analytics/deliverability`). Every report covers the
whole workspace for the period you pick.

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Beginner &nbsp;·&nbsp; Numbers roll up daily; a few cards are
  marked **Updated nightly**.
</Info>

## Before you start

- You need a role with the analytics permission — **Owner**, **Admin**,
  **Manager**, or **Viewer** (Agents see the [Dashboard](/analytics/dashboard) but
  not these reports).
- Dates follow your workspace timezone. Use `/analytics/overview` for the
  headline numbers and drill into a domain report when something looks off.

## Pick a period

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a preset" icon="calendar">
    Open the **Period** selector: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**,
    **Last 30 days** (default), or **This month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or set a custom range" icon="calendar-range">
    Use the date-range picker next to it. Custom ranges can span up to 3 years.
    The selection is stored in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

KPI cards show the change **vs previous period** (a window of equal length
ending where yours starts). Charts and tables show the current period only.

## What each report shows

### Overview

- **KPI cards:** **New Leads**, **Audience Growth** (new contacts minus opt-outs),
  **Messages Sent** (with the share delivered), **Appointments Booked**,
  **Conversions** (with total value), **New Customers**, **First Response Time**,
  and **AI Resolution Rate**.
- **Business health:** a 0–100 score and band (**Excellent**, **Good**, **Needs
  attention**, **At risk**) built from six weighted parts — **Message delivery**,
  **Replies and clicks**, **Audience growth**, **Response speed**, **AI usage**,
  and **Setup progress**. Weak parts get a fix-it link (for example, to
  **Channels** or the setup checklist). This card always covers the trailing 30
  days, regardless of the period selector.
- **AI business coach:** a short narrative plus recommendations generated each
  night for the previous day from your own metrics. New workspaces see **Your
  coach is warming up — check back tomorrow**.
- **Lifecycle funnel:** contacts added in the period by the furthest stage they
  reached — **New leads → Engaged → Appointment booked → Customer**.
- **Channel performance:** **Delivered**, **Replies**, and **Clicks** for
  **Text messages** and **Email** (email adds **Opens**).
- **Campaign performance:** a sortable table (**Name**, **Channel**, **Status**,
  **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Replies**, **Clicks**, **CTR %**, **Created**) with an
  **All / Text / Email** toggle, 20 rows per page, and **Export CSV**.

### Marketing

**Messages Sent**, **Replies**, **Link Clicks**, and **Audience Growth** KPIs; a
daily **Messages, replies & clicks** trend; **Audience sources** (where contacts
came from — **Text-to-Join**, **Signup Form**, **Lead Form**, **Google Ads**,
**Meta Ads**, **CSV Import**, **Added manually**, and conversation sources); and
the same campaign performance table as Overview.

### Conversations

**Conversations Started**, **Conversations Closed**, **First Response Time**, and
**AI Resolution Rate** (the last two are **Updated nightly**); a **Replies
received** trend; **AI vs. your team** (closed conversations **Handled by AI** vs
**Handed to your team**); and **Busiest times of day** — inbound messages by
hour in your workspace timezone.

### Revenue

- **KPIs:** **Conversions**, **Revenue** (split into **From bookings**, **Marked
  won**, and **Workflow goals**), **Estimated send cost**, **ROI**, and **Cost per
  conversion**. ROI shows **—** when a campaign has no recorded send cost.
- **Revenue by source:** **Leads**, **Conversions**, and **Revenue** per
  acquisition source.
- **Campaigns:** attributed **Sends**, **Est. cost**, **Conversions**, **Revenue**,
  **Cost / conversion**, and **ROI** per campaign, 10 per page, plus a **Not linked
  to a campaign** row. Attribution is last-touch: the sale is credited to the last
  campaign that reached the contact in the 30 days before they converted.

Conversions come from completed appointments with a service value, from
**Mark as won** on a contact (enter an optional **Sale amount** and click
**Record win**), from workflow goals, and from an [opportunity](/crm/opportunities)
entering a won stage.

<Note>
  **Estimated send cost** is derived from message volume. For exact charges, see
  [Wallet & billing](/billing/wallet-billing).
</Note>

### Appointments

**Booked**, **Completed**, **Show rate**, **No-show rate**, and **Cancellation
rate**; a **Bookings, completions & no-shows** trend; and three breakdown tables —
**Meeting types**, **Booking sources** (**Booked by AI**, **Booking page**, UTM
campaigns, **Other**), and **Hosts** — each with **Booked**, **Completed**,
**No-show**, and **Show rate**.

<Note>
  Appointments not marked otherwise within 12 hours are assumed attended. You can
  correct any appointment later from [Bookings](/scheduling/bookings).
</Note>

### Deliverability

- **KPIs:** **SMS delivery rate**, **Email delivery rate**, **Opt-out rate**,
  **Spam report rate**, and **Compliance blocked** (texts held back because a
  sender wasn't registered).
- **SMS failures:** undelivered texts by **Reason**, with the raw provider code,
  **Count**, and **% of sent**.
- **Email funnel:** **Processed → Delivered → Deferred → Bounced → Blocked →
  Dropped → Spam reports → Unsubscribes**, a **Bounce breakdown** (permanent vs
  temporary failures), and a **Not receiving email** suppression list.
- **Opt-outs:** daily trend, **By channel**, and **Top keywords**.
- **Sending numbers — 10DLC:** each SMS sender's **Brand** and **Registration
  status** with a pill — **Approved to send**, **Registration in progress**, or
  **Blocked — registration needed** — and a **Fix registration in settings** link.
  See [A2P 10DLC](/compliance/a2p-10dlc).

## Export campaign performance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    On **Overview** or **Marketing**, set the period and the **All / Text / Email**
    toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    The file contains **Name, Channel, Status, Created, Sent, Delivered, Replies,
    Clicks, CTR %** for up to 5,000 campaigns in the period, newest first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only the campaign performance table exports today. Other cards and tables have
  no download.
</Note>

## Weekly digest email

Every Monday, members with analytics access receive an email summarizing the
previous week (subject: *Your week at &lt;workspace&gt;: N new leads, N bookings*)
with the same AI-written recommendations. Each email has a one-click unsubscribe
link in the footer.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't the Overview and Conversations numbers match my inbox right now?">
    First response time and AI resolution rate are recalculated overnight, so
    today's conversations appear tomorrow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter by campaign or segment?">
    Not across the reports. The campaign tables filter by channel; per-campaign
    detail lives in [Campaign analytics](/analytics/campaign-analytics).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Business health different from my selected period?">
    It is a current-state gauge and always uses the last 30 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does ad spend or attribution show up?">
    Lead sources from ads appear under **Audience sources** and **Revenue by
    source**. Ad platform attribution is covered in
    [Ad attribution](/analytics/ad-attribution).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A card says “Couldn't load these numbers”">
    Click **Retry**. Reports are rate-limited per user; wait a moment if you
    refreshed many times in quick succession.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Revenue shows conversions but the Campaigns table is empty">
    Those conversions had no campaign touch in the prior 30 days — they sit in the
    **Not linked to a campaign** row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Compliance blocked is climbing">
    Open **Deliverability → Sending numbers — 10DLC** and fix any sender marked
    **Blocked — registration needed**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
