## Overview

The WhatsApp channel connects a **WhatsApp Business** number to Autocampaign AI so
customers can message you on WhatsApp and you can reply from the
[Inbox](/inbox/overview), send from [workflows](/automation/workflow-builder) and
[sequences](/campaigns/sequences), or let an [AI Employee](/automation/ai-employees)
answer. Connect it under **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp**
(`/settings/channels/whatsapp`); manage templates under **Templates → WhatsApp**
(`/templates/whatsapp`).

<Info>
  **Difficulty:** Intermediate &nbsp;·&nbsp; Connecting needs channel-management
  permission; templates need the **WhatsApp templates** permissions.
</Info>

## Before you start

- A Meta Business account with a **WhatsApp Business Account** and a phone number
  registered to it. One connection (one number) per workspace.
- Pop-ups allowed — the connection runs in a Meta window.

## Connect WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the WhatsApp card" icon="plug">
    Go to **Settings → Channels**, find **WhatsApp** under **Meta Platforms**, and
    click **Configure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with WhatsApp" icon="right-to-bracket">

    You'll see your business name, number, a health badge (**Connected**, **Needs
    attention**, or **Disconnected**), and a **Manage WhatsApp templates** link.

## The 24-hour window and consent

Every inbound WhatsApp message opens a **24-hour customer-service window** for that
contact and records them as opted in to WhatsApp.

- **Window open** — send free-form text (up to 4,096 characters) and media.
- **Window closed** (no inbound message in the last 24 hours, or none ever) — only an
  **approved template** can be sent. That's how you start or restart a conversation.
- **Opted out** — a contact who replies with an opt-out keyword (for example `STOP`,
  as set under **Settings → Opt-out**) can't be messaged on WhatsApp at all, templates
  included. Their `STOP` still appears in the thread.

<Note>
  A WhatsApp `STOP` doesn't silence SMS (though an SMS opt-out also blocks WhatsApp),
  and it's sticky — there's currently no switch on the contact record to opt them back
  in.
</Note>

Templates are pre-written messages Meta reviews and approves. Open **Templates →
WhatsApp**; the grid filters by **All**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved**, and
**Rejected**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Purpose" icon="bullseye">
    Click **Create template** and pick a purpose: **Appointment reminder**, **Order
    update**, **Welcome message**, **Follow-up** (all Utility), **Promotion**
    (Marketing), or **Other**, where you choose the **Meta category** yourself. Only
    Marketing and Utility are offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Editor" icon="pen">
    Enter a **Template name** and **Language code** (default `en_US`), then write the
    **Message body** (up to 1,024 characters). Use **Personalize** to insert merge
    tags such as `{{first_name}}`; add a fallback with `{{first_name|"there"}}`.
    Optionally add a **Header** and **Footer** (60 characters each) and up to three
    **Buttons** — **Quick reply** or **Website link**. Merge tags work only in the body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review" icon="eye">
    Check the phone preview, then **Save draft** or **Submit for review**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Statuses run **Draft → Under review → Approved / Rejected**; Meta can later mark a
template **Paused** or **Disabled**. Only drafts and rejected templates (which show
Meta's reason) can be edited, then resubmitted. Click **Sync status** to pull the
latest approval state. Built-in **System** templates can't be edited — **Clone to
use** copies one into your workspace; **Duplicate** copies your own.

## Reply and compose in the inbox

Inbound WhatsApp messages land in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview) on the contact's
**WhatsApp** channel tab; unknown numbers become a new contact named "Unknown". Text,
images, and documents arrive with captions; other types show a placeholder such as
`[Voice message]`. While the window is open the composer shows **You can reply
normally**. When it's closed, click **Choose a template**, pick one, and click
**Send template**.

To start a conversation, open the inbox's **Message composer** menu and choose
**WhatsApp** (shown only when connected). In **Compose WhatsApp**, add up to 50
existing contacts under **To**, then write a **Message** or pick a **Template**. If
any recipient's window is closed the panel switches to template mode, and opted-out
contacts are listed so you can **Remove** them. The **WhatsApp** quick action on a
contact's page or drawer opens the same composer.

## Workflows, sequences, and AI Employees

- **Send WhatsApp** (workflows) and the **WhatsApp** step (sequences) send free-form
  text. Set **If WhatsApp can't message this contact right now** to **Skip this step**
  or **Send an approved template instead** with a **Fallback template**.
- **Send WhatsApp Template** / **WhatsApp Template** sends an approved template
  directly. Publishing is blocked while a step references an unapproved template.
- Both steps respect quiet hours and skip contacts who opted out of WhatsApp.
- **AI Employees** reply on WhatsApp with WhatsApp-style formatting. If the window
  closes before the reply goes out, the run is logged as skipped (WhatsApp window
  closed), not as an error; the AI never sends templates on its own.

<Note>
  WhatsApp isn't a campaign channel yet — use sequences or workflows for automated
  WhatsApp sends.
</Note>

## Status and limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Connected numbers | 1 per workspace |
| Free-form message | 4,096 characters |
| Template body / header / footer | 1,024 / 60 / 60 characters |
| Template buttons | 3, labels up to 25 characters |
| Compose recipients | 50 contacts per send |
| Outbound media | Images (`jpg`, `png`, `webp`, `gif`) or documents |

Delivery shows as **Sent** or **Delivered** (read receipts count as delivered). Sends
and templates count toward your [plan's allowances](/billing/plans-and-trials).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I type a reply?">
    The 24-hour window has closed. Use **Choose a template**; a reply from the contact
    reopens the window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I message a number that isn't a contact?">
    No — WhatsApp compose accepts existing contacts only, because a new number has no
    consent state to check.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The connect window failed or timed out">
    Allow pop-ups, check the number belongs to the selected WhatsApp Business Account,
    and click **Connect with WhatsApp** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A template with this name and language already exists">
    Names are unique per language in your workspace — rename it or change the
    **Language code**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A workflow step was skipped">
    The contact opted out, or the window was closed and the step is set to **Skip this
    step**. Add a **Fallback template** to send instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
